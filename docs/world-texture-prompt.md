Authoring mode: built-in image generation. The source photos are architectural references; the game uses the new texture atlas rather than projecting panorama images onto buildings.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production diffuse texture atlas for a PS2-inspired Beirut open-world game, square 2048x2048 pixels.
Create ONE clean borderless 4 by 4 atlas of exactly 16 equally sized square texture cells, edge-to-edge with NO gutters, labels, captions, grid outlines or padding. Every cell must be entirely filled. This is a flat orthographic texture sheet, NOT a scene, collage of photos, mockup or perspective illustration.
The supplied street photographs are architectural/material REFERENCE ONLY. Author new painted game textures matching Beirut's worn local materials. Never reproduce Google logos/watermarks, photographic people, cars, sky or perspective. Soft diffuse albedo with painted small crevice detail, no hard directional lighting or cast shadows.
Cell order, row-major from top left:
ROW 1 — four DIFFERENT seamlessly repeatable apartment façade modules, each showing exactly 4 window bays across and 4 floors vertically, with floor lines ending seamlessly at boundaries:
1. warm ivory worn plaster, narrow dark teal windows, thin concrete sills, modest green shutters, occasional small air conditioners.
2. honey limestone Lebanese facade, graceful arched windows, aged blue-green shutters, fine stone joints.
3. weathered pale grey modern concrete apartments, inset dark windows and narrow metal balcony railings; electrical cabling and AC boxes.
4. muted dusty salmon painted plaster apartments, white trim, brown shutters and shallow balconies.
ROW 2 — four ground-floor storefront textures, one level each, matching the four façade palettes above, all FRONT-ON and edge-to-edge. Retail doorways and windows, roller shutters, striped awnings and restrained signage integrated architecturally. No people/vehicles/pavement/sky. Signs: cell5 "بقالة"; cell6 "قهوة"; cell7 "SERVICE"; cell8 "مكتبة". Door thresholds at bottom edge and lintel/awning at top. Interior darkness behind windows, worn shop paint. Each storefront cell should repeat horizontally plausibly.
ROW 3 — 4 seamless TOP-DOWN/flat material swatches, with no horizon or perspective:
9. dark charcoal asphalt, fine aggregate, subtle patched repairs and hairline cracks, absolutely NO road markings.
10. warm light-beige square sidewalk paving with fine dirt in joints, repeatable grid.
11. flat weathered grey concrete rooftop, waterproofing repairs, faint expansion seams and drain details.
12. light grey bridge/curb concrete with weathering, subtle aggregate and fine expansion joints.
ROW 4 — 4 seamless material swatches:
13. old rough honey-grey limestone retaining-wall blocks, front-on staggered horizontal masonry.
14. terracotta roof tiles, top-down repeating curved tile pattern.
15. patchy muted Mediterranean grass and dry soil, top-down ground texture, no blades sticking outside cell.
16. Mediterranean blue-green water albedo, top-down subdued ripple pattern, no horizon, foam or reflected objects.
Style: hand-painted but grounded, readable low-resolution early-2000s game diffuse textures, restrained natural colors, medium contrast, crisp architectural shapes. Texture material detail, not pixel-art outlines. All sixteen cells exactly occupy their own quarter-width and quarter-height rectangle and have tileable edges appropriate to their material.
```
